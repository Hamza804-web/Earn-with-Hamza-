Stay safe online

A few important checks before joining an online earning platform.

Check first

Be careful with deposits

Be suspicious of platforms that require payment to unlock a job or promise fixed, unusually high returns.

Protect yourself

Keep personal details private

Never share passwords, one-time codes or banking PINs with strangers or unknown websites.

Verify

Check the source

Look for clear company details, independent reviews and understandable terms before using a service.
